Start small and keep experimenting

You don't have to be an expert to try programming. Start with a simple idea, like a quiz, a personal webpage, or a tiny game.

  1. Choose a language or web technology that interests you.
  2. Follow a beginner tutorial and try the examples.
  3. Make a small project and improve it as you learn.

When you get an error, don't give up. Read the message, check your code, and try to figure out what happened.
